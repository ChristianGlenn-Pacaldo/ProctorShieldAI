ProctorShield AI — Current Use-Case Model

As-built functional baseline • Version 3.0 • September 2026

ProctorShield AI System
🎓

Student

  • Register, sign in, and recover a password
  • Join with an access code and wait for teacher start
  • Take a responsive desktop/mobile quiz
  • Grant camera consent and receive device assurance
  • Save answers, submit, and view results
  • Request an allowed retake
🧑‍🏫

Teacher

  • Create manual or Gemini-assisted quizzes
  • Activate, start, manage, and end quiz sessions
  • Monitor students and receive violation alerts
  • Play or download protected incident evidence
  • Review grades, AI reports, and integrity verdicts
  • Approve/reject retakes and manage Pro billing
🛡️

Administrator

  • Manage users and account status
  • Review platform analytics and activity logs
  • Inspect active quizzes and violations
  • Administer subscriptions and plans
  • Monitor presence and system readiness
  • Maintain retention and operational settings
↓ ↓ ↓
Core Functional Services
Identity, Recovery & RBACJWT HttpOnly sessions, Google OAuth, email OTP recovery, session revocation, and strict role/ownership checks.
Quiz Lifecycle & AttemptsDraft → Active → In Progress → Ended, waiting lobby, autosave, server grading, and immutable numbered retakes.
Browser-Side Proctoringface-api.js landmarks, COCO-SSD phone detection, focus/fullscreen/clipboard/shortcut/audio signals, and a three-strike limit.
Evidence & Live MonitoringRedis-backed live snapshots, Pusher metadata alerts, and protected downloadable 3–5 second incident videos in object storage.
Subscriptions & EntitlementsFive lifetime manual quizzes on Free; unlimited quizzes, AI Create, Live Monitor, Evidence Replay, and reports on Pro.
Notifications & ReportingPersistent role-scoped notification destinations, dashboard navigation, scores, integrity summaries, and AI-assisted verdicts.
Controlled External Services
Gemini AIQuiz generation and post-submission verdict assistance; not continuous webcam analysis.
PayMongo — Test ModeCheckout, signed/idempotent webhooks, persistent Pro activation, expiry, and refund handling.
Neon • Redis • S3 • PusherRelational persistence, shared transient state, durable evidence, and lightweight real-time events.
Assurance boundary: browser software cannot guarantee detection of every operating-system screenshot, external camera, hardware capture device, or mobile gesture. Supported mobile sessions are explicitly labeled Reduced Assurance.
